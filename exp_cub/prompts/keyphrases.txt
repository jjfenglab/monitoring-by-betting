You will be given an image of a bird. Extract all visual descriptors of the bird. These will be candidates for a central dictionary of descriptors for characterizing bird images, and will eventually be input features to a concept bottleneck model for monitoring changes in the birds photographed over time. Only list characteristics visible in the image. Do not infer or add details that are not visible.

For each descriptor of the bird, provide:
1. A word or phrase to represent that descriptor (1-2 words)
2. Synonyms or alternative phrasings

Requirements:
- Each descriptor should be at most 2 words. One word descriptors are encouraged.
- Output characteristics that this bird possesses, such as "long tail" or "red eye". Every item must be a characteristic the bird has, never the name of an attribute: do not output "eye color", "tail length", "head shape", "wing coloration", "feet type", "body size", or any other category label, and do not output a body part on its own such as "tail" or "beak".
- Separate items with commas
- Output at least 10 keyphrases

Do not infer or fabricate details. Base all responses strictly on what is visible in the image.

Output Format:
{
    "reasoning": "Describe any reasoning here",
    "keyphrases": [
        "Primary descriptor, Synonym1, Synonym2",
        "Primary descriptor, Alternative phrasing",
        ...
    ]
}

Examples:
{
    "reasoning": "Describe any reasoning here",
    "keyphrases": [
        "long tail, elongated tail",
        "hooked beak, curved bill, hooked bill",
        ...
    ]
}
